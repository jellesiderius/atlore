# Account and Preferences

Open the round account button on the campaign overview to manage your account.

## Profile

You can change:

- display name;
- email address;
- profile colour.

The profile colour identifies you in avatars and realtime collaboration cursors. Choose **Save account** after editing these fields.

## Change your password

1. Enter your current password.
2. Enter a new password of at least 10 characters.
3. Repeat the new password.
4. Choose **Save password**.

Changing the password signs out other active sessions for account security.

## Appearance

Choose **Dark** or **Light** under **Preferences**. The preference is stored in the browser and applies immediately.

## Language

Choose **NL** for Dutch or **EN** for English. Atlore stores the selection in both browser storage and a cookie so the interface, server errors, invitations, and recovery emails use the same language.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** at the bottom of the account menu. On shared devices, always sign out instead of merely closing the browser tab.

## Password recovery

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Atlore sends a short-lived recovery link when SMTP is configured. A self-hosted development environment without SMTP writes the recovery message to the application logs instead.
